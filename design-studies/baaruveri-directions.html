Baaruveri ބާރުވެރި — three direction studies

Three differentiated visual directions for the Baaruveri civic platform, each from a different design school. Six primitives — atlas index card, island profile header, thread card, claim card, petition card, data-table strip — built identically in each, so visual differences are isolated. Real Maafaru content. Real Maldivian names. Bilingual Dhivehi RTL primary + English. Post-research-aware: no serif body type in any direction.

A · Saafu ސާފު B · Sukoonu ސުކޫނު C · Dhirun ދިރުން

A · Saafu ސާފު

Information architecture · Pentagram · Tufte · USDS data work

archival · monumental · transparent · sourced · sober

Civic data is the hero; the UI is a chassis for tables, sparklines, year-over-year comparisons. Reads as "the official record, made browsable" — the kind of platform a journalist trusts as a source citation. Tabular numerals dominant, footnoted, hairline rules, single restrained accent.

B · Sukoonu ސުކޫނު

Eastern minimalism · Naoto Fukasawa · Sou Fujimoto · Govt.nz restraint

hospitable · dignified · quiet · trustworthy · participable

When civic space is "obstructed," a shouty platform feels coercive — citizens flinch. A quiet platform invites participation by refusing to perform. Generous whitespace, a single restrained accent (lacquer indigo), type contrast through size not weight, almost no borders. Dhivehi and English at visual parity throughout — neither feels secondary.

C · Dhirun ދިރުން

Motion poetics · Field.io · Stripe Press · Bloomberg calm-mode · Cheong Wa Dae counter

alive · instrumented · responsive · urgent-when-it-needs-to-be · modern

Civic data is moving — petition signatures tick up, fiscal-year spends progress, erosion advances. Motion expresses life, not animation. The platform feels like the country is breathing through the screen, useful when you want citizens to feel a 5,000-signature threshold instead of seeing a static fraction. Counter-tape and sparkline language pervasive; Bloomberg-for-citizens, not dashboard-for-execs.
