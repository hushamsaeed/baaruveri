Baaruveri ބާރުވެރި — top-nav directions

Three differentiated layout mechanics for the global top nav, all in the locked Saafu visual language. Same primitives in each: wordmark, 5 nav links, LocaleSwitcher, auth chip. Each direction shown in 4 states — LTR unauthed, LTR authed, RTL unauthed, RTL authed — with a thin content-preview strip below so the nav reads in context.

A · Ledger Bar

Pentagram annual reports · Foundation Center · USAspending

A single 56px hairline-bound row. Wordmark on the start, nav links inline at the end, LocaleSwitcher and auth chip in an end-cluster separated by a vertical hairline. The most condensed, editorial reading — like a journal masthead. Active item gets a 2px gazette-teal underline that rhymes with the table-header rule downstream.
